  • Politics and Public Affairs: Assessment of the Prioritization of the Mediterranean Region

    This policy memo provides evidence to support the prioritization of the Mediterranean region through the historical and strategic significance of the Mediterranean with regard to U.S. interests across Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa.

  • INTL400: The Eagle and the Condor: U.S. Intervention and Hemispheric Conformity in Cold War Latin America

    This paper examines U.S. intervention in Latin America during the Cold War, focusing on Operation Condor and the enforcement of anti-communist ideology. Using declassified CIA documents and quantitative data, it identifies key patterns of American involvement and analyzes how the U.S. shaped regional conformity through intelligence and military strategies.

    Read the full research paper (Google Drive)
